## James
- accreted globular clusters formed in smaller satellite galaxies that are then consumed by a larger galaxy
- "Accreted globular clusters should produce weakly bound systems in which age and metallicity are correlated with eachother but inversely correlated with specific orbital energy." - https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2021AJ....162...42W/abstract or https://arxiv.org/html/2104.10697v1 
- clusters moving differently to the majority of the galaxy in which they exist; signals that they are of external origin
- "... confirm previous findings that the blue, metal-poor clusters form in satellite galaxies that are accreted on to the Milky Way, while the red, metal-rich clusters form mostly in situ, or, to a lower extent, in massive, self-enriched galaxies merging with the Milky Way." - https://www.astro.lu.se/~oscar/publication/fa78914c-6b62-49f6-bb08-4905605218cd
- Bifurcated AMR (Age-Metallicity Relation); this article may be the key: https://researchportalplus.anu.edu.au/en/publications/the-bifurcated-age-metallicity-relation-of-milky-way-globular-clu/
